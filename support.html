JONA Support

Help with connecting your iPhone, sharing your screen and adjusting the picture while safely parked.

Contact the developer

Contact support

GitHub issues are public. Share only a general problem description. Never post passwords, private screenshots, access tokens or unreviewed diagnostics.

Get started

  1. Park safely and connect your iPhone to CarPlay.
  2. Open JONA on the vehicle display and confirm parked mode.
  3. On your iPhone, tap the large Play button and approve the iOS screen-sharing picker.
  4. Open your source video app. Select the vehicle as that app’s audio output.
  5. To finish, tap the Stop square in JONA, use Stop on the car toolbar, or end sharing through the system control.

Adjust the picture

Tap the car picture to reveal the toolbar. Auto fits portrait and fills landscape; Fit shows the full image; Fill covers the app’s display area and may crop edges. When vertical cropping is present, drag the picture vertically to adjust the visible area. Changing image mode recenters the picture.

Common questions

Play asks me to connect the car

Connect CarPlay and open JONA on the vehicle display before starting sharing. The phone’s sharing indicator alone does not prove that the vehicle is receiving video.

There is no sound or sound comes from the phone

Choose the vehicle as the output in your source app or the system audio-output picker. JONA’s direct sharing path does not play a second copy of captured audio.

The picture is sideways, upside down or too small

Try Auto or Fill and rotate the source video. If it remains incorrect, note the phone orientation, source app and JONA version and contact support. A software update may be required; compatibility is not guaranteed for every vehicle.

A video is black or sharing stops

Protected content may block capture. JONA cannot bypass content protection or system restrictions. If the system ends sharing, start again using the system approval flow.

Trials, Full Access and restoring purchases

In versions offering paid access, the free trial lasts seven calendar days from the original trial confirmation with Apple. It does not renew or charge you automatically. After the trial, sharing requires a separate one-time Full Access purchase at the price shown in the app for your storefront. Vehicle compatibility and content restrictions still apply.

Use Restore Purchases on the access screen with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoring a trial preserves its original start date. If products cannot load, check your connection, use Retry and verify your App Store account. Local development configurations may not behave like the released App Store version. Apple handles purchases and refund requests through Report a Problem.

Diagnostics

On the iPhone, open Help → Diagnostics. If support requests a report, reproduce the issue while safely parked and connected, then choose Export Diagnostic Report. Review the report and destination before sharing. Reports do not contain captured pictures or audio, but can contain device, route and error details.

What to include in a support request

  • JONA version and iOS version.
  • Vehicle make, model/year and wired or wireless connection.
  • Steps to reproduce, expected result and actual result.
  • Whether the problem affects picture, orientation or sound.

Compatibility and safe use

JONA currently targets iOS 27 or later. Availability depends on the OS, vehicle and supported output. Use video only while safely parked and follow applicable laws and vehicle restrictions. JONA does not supply a media catalogue or bypass protected content.
