Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Developer: Ugurcan Yagiz

This policy explains how JONA handles information when you use the iPhone app, these support pages, or contact the developer.

Your screen stays under your control.

JONA does not require a JONA account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. The current app does not automatically upload screen content or diagnostic reports to the developer.

Screen sharing and audio

With your approval through the iOS sharing picker, JONA processes live screen frames in memory and sends them to the connected vehicle display. The direct sharing feature does not save a screen recording or upload those frames to a developer-operated server. Screen content may include personal information visible on your phone; check what is on screen before sharing and stop sharing when finished.

Your source app plays audio through its selected output. JONA does not replay captured audio in its direct display path and does not record the microphone. The operating system controls sharing permissions. You can end sharing using JONA’s Stop control or the system sharing control.

Information stored on your device

JONA stores your image-mode preference and a bounded diagnostic event history locally. Diagnostics can include hardware model, iOS version, app version/build, timestamps, connection and audio-route status, frame dimensions/orientation, and error or performance information. These reports do not include captured screen images or audio.

The event history is limited to the latest 100 events; when loading saved events on launch, entries older than 24 hours are excluded. A report you choose to export is written to temporary app storage and presented in the system share sheet. Reports and copies saved outside JONA remain subject to the destination you select. Uninstalling the app removes its local app-container data; copies you exported and any device backups are managed separately.

App Store purchases and trial access

Versions that offer a trial or Full Access use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit to process purchases. JONA checks Apple-verified purchase records on your device, including the product identifier, original purchase date and current entitlement status, to determine trial duration and paid access. These records are not automatically uploaded to the developer. The developer does not receive your payment-card or bank details through JONA.

Apple processes purchase and Apple Account information under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Apple may provide the developer with sales and financial reports through App Store Connect. Restoring purchases uses your Apple Account; reinstalling JONA does not necessarily remove your Apple purchase history or restart a trial.

Sharing diagnostics and contacting support

No diagnostic report is automatically sent to the developer. If you choose to share a report or submit a support request, the recipient receives the information you provide. Common credential patterns are scrubbed from exported reports, but automated scrubbing cannot guarantee removal of every sensitive value. Review a report before sending it.

Support is available through GitHub Issues. GitHub may require an account to submit an issue. Issues, usernames, messages and attachments in this public repository are publicly visible. Do not post passwords, access tokens, personal screen captures, private messages or unreviewed diagnostic reports. You can start with a general description and ask how to proceed.

Network requests and third parties

The optional sample-video test requests media hosted by Apple. Apple and network providers may receive network information such as your IP address when that media is requested. Source video apps, the operating system and vehicle systems have their own data practices.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses, request details and support-account information under its Privacy Statement. These pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising cookies, contact forms or third-party fonts. GitHub’s infrastructure and services may process information in other countries.

Use, retention and your choices

The developer uses information you voluntarily provide to answer requests and troubleshoot JONA, not for advertising or selling personal information. Support records remain on GitHub until removed, subject to GitHub’s retention, backups and policies. To request access, correction or removal of information you supplied to the developer, contact the developer through the support channel without including sensitive details in the initial public request. Rights and available remedies depend on your location; GitHub account or hosting data requests should also be directed to GitHub where appropriate.

Children

JONA is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If such information has been submitted to support, contact the developer to request removal.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the described practices change, with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, contact Ugurcan Yagiz using JONA support on GitHub.
